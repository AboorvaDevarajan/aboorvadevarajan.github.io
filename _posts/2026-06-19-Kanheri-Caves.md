**19 June.** The day before, [we had walked through South Mumbai](/blog/2026/06/18/South-Mumbai/) until we reached the harbour. Now we went the other way, into Sanjay Gandhi National Park. The streets gave way to forest, and a basalt hill rose ahead.

The board at the bottom of the steps says Kanheri. The caves here run from the 1st century BCE to about the 10th century. The sculptures came later, with most of the Buddhas from the 5th and 6th centuries. Monks lived here across all of that time. Modern Mumbai is still just past the trees.

The last part of this Mumbai journey goes back to the water. The sea had been at the edge of these days all along: beautiful from the promenade, full of garbage at Juhu, and one long coastline from the plane. [The story ends with the sea and Juhu](/blog/2026/06/20/The-sea-and-Juhu/).
